Game Mode setup

Five quick steps so Pokémon GO trusts the location. Coordinates go over the USB cable; Bluetooth is what makes the phone treat them as coming from a real GPS accessory.

1
Connect the iPhone by USB Use a data cable and trust this computer if asked.
…
2
Pair Bluetooth with this PC iPhone → Settings → Bluetooth → your PC's name → Pair. On iOS 18+ connect it from the Control Centre Bluetooth panel too. If pairing fails, ignore the device on both sides and retry.
…
3
Connect the GPS accessory Starts the Bluetooth accessory session. On the phone, tap “Pair/Connect” if a prompt appears.
…
4
Refresh the phone's location On the iPhone: toggle Airplane Mode on, count to 10, off again — it re-arms the radios cleanly in one go. Then check your Wi-Fi reconnected (Settings → Wi-Fi → tap your network): the Control Centre Wi-Fi toggle disconnects but does not rejoin, and the phone will silently use cellular instead. Location Services itself should stay ON.
•
5
Open Pokémon GO Give it up to 30 s. No Error 12 means you're in. Keep the cable and this window open while playing.
